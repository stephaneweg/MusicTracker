**Design rules (user-mandated, OBLIGATORY):** a **4-level generative DAG** = N1 form/phrase→tonality · N2 harmony→harmonic-rhythm · N3 melody(coupled)→melodic-rhythm + texture · N4 dynamics·articulation·voice-leading. Each dim learns a CONDITIONAL dist `P(state | own-history, parent context)`. Three guardrails to beat sparsity (~100 pieces): (1) **low order** — order 1 everywhere, order 2 ONLY on the harmony ROOT chain; (2) **abstract states** — degrees/intervals (never absolute pitch), duration/register CLASSES; factor secondary attrs (quality, rhythm) into their own low-order tables; (3) **backoff/smoothing** — every table keeps a back-off ladder (specific→marginal), runtime interpolates Katz/Witten-Bell. **Harmony FACTORED** = root-motion chain (order 2) + `P(quality|degree,mode)` (order 0). **Melody lean** = `P(nextDeg | prevDeg × chordFunc(T/S/D/X) × isChordTone)` → backoff; rhythm & phrase live in SEPARATE tables. Two explicit melodic-interval side-chains (per mode): `IntervalByRhythm` = `P(|interval|class | rhythmClass)` (size given rhythm) and `IntervalByDegree` = `P(SIGNED interval | current degree)` (direction+size given the note → contour/tendency-tone resolution; e.g. minor 7→+step, #4→+step, b7→+step, 3→-step). Tonic/mode = **Krumhansl-Schmuckler on the EXACT symbolic pitch-class histogram** (better than WAV/audio chroma since corpus is symbolic).

**Validation (from-scratch reproduces the known template):** minor melody pentatonic {1,b3,4,5,b7}=74% / major {1,2,3,5,6}=70%, tendency tones (b2,#4,7,3) avoided ~tiny; chord-tone 72%(min)/79%(maj); harmony plagal+modal-mediant (4→1, 5→1, b3/b6/b7 motion), **V mostly triad/sus NOT dom7**, b6/b3→maj7 lush mediants; **1 chord/bar = 86%**; leap reverses 60% vs step 30%; 4/4=87%; voice motion oblique 46% (pedal bass under moving melody), mel↔bass mostly 8ve+ apart, doubling ~2% (independent voices); modulation offsets dom/+m3(=rel major)/whole-step.

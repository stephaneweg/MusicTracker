Full Bach/Vivaldi-level analysis of `midi/other/ex_class2*.mid` (4 variants; `ex_class2_c.mid` = the full 97-bar piece, others are reductions). Tools: analyze_sonata.ps1 (windowed K-S key map + per-bar chord order) and cadence_vl.ps1 (V→i voice-leading), both kept at repo root.

- **KEY**: the user guessed G minor; the analysis shows **C MINOR** (the prominent "G" is the DOMINANT V; G minor appears only in the development). Global K-S blurs to C major because of the raised leading tone. (Could be a transposition of an original in G minor — the relationships are what matter.)
- **FORM = sonata** (confirmed): windowed tonal map = **Exposition b0-15 in i (Cm) → transition → V (G major) b16; EXPOSITION REPEAT b24-47 (Cm→G); DEVELOPMENT b48-71 modulating through Em, Eb major (relative III), G minor (v); RECAP b72-96 back in Cm.** Classic i→V exposition, relative-key/dominant excursion, return to i.
- **HARMONY / chord order** (1st theme): `i – vii° – V – i` (tonic+dominant/leading-tone), then `i – III – i – ♭II – ii – V` (note the **♭II = NEAPOLITAN**), with structural pillars **I held ~8 beats** and **V held ~6 beats** before cadences, and **II (=V/V secondary dominant) → V**. Colours: ♭II (Neapolitan), ♭VII, III. Harmonic rhythm mostly 1-2 beats/chord.
- **CADENCE voice-leading** (the user's key interest — "notes conjointes de la quinte vers l'accord final"): over 16 V→i resolutions the **SOPRANO moves by STEP 75%** (avg 2.1 semitones) — 7̂→1̂ (leading tone up a semitone) or 2̂→1̂ (down a tone) — while the bass leaps the V→i root (4th/5th). The conjunct melodic resolution IS the cadential signature.
- **RHYTHM**: clean classical pulse — eighths 56% + quarters 33%, q-q-q dominant; minimal rests (3% silence, legato, quarter-rests on beats). Melody + an Alberti/arpeggiated bass (2 voices); the bass leaps (Alberti).

Grounded in online theory (MasterClass/Britannica sonata, Lumen rondo, teoria fugue). Forms:
- **Sonate** (44 bars): A antecedent→V (Demi cad) · A consequent→I · transition · 2nd theme in **dominant (major) / relative major (minor)** · development (related key) · recap A · recap 2nd theme **in tonic**. Verified C minor → i / Eb (relative) / i / tonic.

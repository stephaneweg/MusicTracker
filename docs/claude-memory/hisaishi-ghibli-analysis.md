**MELODY** (skyline of the top track; mono solo lines cross-check):
- **PENTATONIC.** Relative to the tonic, **78% of melodic weight sits on just 5 notes {1, b3, 4, 5, b7}** (= minor pentatonic on T = major pentatonic on T+3 — same 5 notes, hence the major/relative-minor AMBIGUITY that defines the sound). The half-step tendency tones (leading-tone 7, b2, #4) total only **3.8%** — they're avoided. Fleshed out to ~natural-minor/Aeolian with 2 (10%) + b6 (6.6%).
- **Mostly stepwise with expressive lyrical leaps.** Clean mono lines (violin/flute/solo) = **step 46–58%**; the piano-skyline aggregate over-counts leaps (top note jumps with the RH chords). Top intervals M2 25%, unison 18% (gentle repeated notes), m2 13%, then **P4 12%, P5 9%, octave 3%** = the soaring leaps at phrase peaks.
- **Strongly MOTIVIC**: 71% of 3-note interval-cells recur (≥2×) — short cell stated, repeated/sequenced. **Balanced wavy contour**: 55% direction-changes (arches, not long runs).
- **Regular phrasing**: median phrase = 4 beats, peaks at 2 and 4 (and 6, 8) beats → square 2-/4-bar phrases, song-like.
- **Rhythm**: melody = 8ths 47% + quarters 21% + 16ths 12% (flowing eighth lyricism, quarter anchors, some triplet/dotted lilt). Accompaniment = **continuous 8ths 57%** (a broken-chord/arpeggiated ostinato under the tune). Mostly **4/4** (a few **3/4** waltzes).
- **Dynamics**: full **ppp–ff (vel 16–112)**, mean mf(75) — wide expressive swells.

**HARMONY** (per-beat chord ID from full texture; heuristic template match — labels approximate, distribution robust):
- **COLOUR over function.** Chord-quality share: triad 45%, **sus2/sus4 17.5%**, **add9 11%**, **maj7/maj9 8.6%**, min7/m9 6%, 6th 3% — i.e. **~40% are colour chords**, while **dominant 7ths are RARE (5.6%)**. Lush/open/suspended sonorities, NOT V7→I tension.
- **MODAL / borrowed chords everywhere**: progressions are saturated with **bVII, bIII, bVI** (Aeolian/Mixolydian mixture) and occasional **#IV / bII** (Lydian/Phrygian colour — the "epic/ancient" Mononoke sound). E.g. *Summer*: I–Vsus4–bVI–bIIIsus4–bVII–IVsus4–bIII (a descending modal chain); *Castle*: bVIIm9–bVImaj9–bIImaj9–IVm7–Vsus4–Isus2–Im.
- **Root motion** is NOT fifth-dominated: fifths 27% (both directions), **thirds/mediants ~22% (+m3/+M3/+M6)**, steps (+M2) 9%, and **static/pedal 22.5%** (same root). Floating, mediant-related harmony.
- **Inversions / pedal / slash bass = 30%** (bass ≠ root) — descending or static bass lines under shifting upper chords.
- **No "royal road" (IV-V-iii-vi) — 0 exact hits.** Cadences are PLAGAL/MODAL (IV–I, bVII–I, sus–I), not dominant.
- **Relative major/minor float**: both major-tonic pieces (Summer, Mononoke = I major + modal mixture) and minor-tonic (Castle, Nostalgia, Name of Life), often shifting within a piece.
